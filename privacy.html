C. DATA SETS WE COLLECT AND FOR WHAT PURPOSE:

Reverse Audio: Sing & Voice FX is built with a strict zero-server, local-first, 100% on-device audio and video processing architecture. We do not require you to register an account or log in. All live microphone recordings, sample-exact PCM audio reversals, 8-preset Voice Morpher DSP effects, 0.5x–2.0x speed adjustments, and video timeline frame reversals are executed locally on your iOS device using Apple’s native AVAudioEngine, AVFoundation, and CoreMedia frameworks. We do not operate backend cloud servers that collect, upload, or store your private voice recordings, imported music files, or personal video clips.

Non-Personal Data

During your interaction with the App, we process non-identifiable studio preference and configuration settings locally on your device (“Non-Personal Data”), such as your selected interface language (across 19 supported locales), light/dark/system appearance mode, preferred audio export format (M4A or WAV), loop playback toggle, auto-save recording preference, and haptic feedback setting.

We do not knowingly collect or process any Special Categories of Personal Data (such as racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, genetic data, biometric voiceprint identification, or health data).
